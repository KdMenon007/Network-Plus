### **7 Steps of Troubleshooting**

1. **Identify the Problem**:
    
    - Understand the symptoms, gather information, and interact with users to define the issue accurately.
        
2. **Establish a Theory of Probable Cause**:
    
    - Formulate potential causes of the issue based on collected information and observations.
        
3. **Test the Theory to Determine the Cause**:
    
    - Apply practical methods to verify whether the hypothesis is correct and pinpoint the root cause.
        
4. **Establish a Plan of Action to Resolve the Problem**:
    
    - Create a solution plan to address the identified cause, considering any potential impacts and consequences of the resolution.
        
5. **Implement the Solution or Escalate as Necessary**:
    
    - Execute the solution and resolve the problem. If the issue is beyond your expertise, escalate it to the appropriate personnel.
        
6. **Verify Full System Functionality**:
    
    - Confirm that the system is fully functional after the resolution and implement preventive measures to avoid future issues.
        
7. **Document Findings, Actions, Outcomes, and Lessons Learned**:
    
    - Record all findings, the steps taken, results, and any lessons learned to aid in future troubleshooting and knowledge sharing.
        

---

### **Step 1: Identify the Problem**

- **Understand Symptoms**:
    
    - Gather detailed information and engage with affected users to accurately define the issue.
        
- **Gather Information**:
    
    - Collect relevant details about the issue from system logs, user reports, and network performance data.
        
- **Question Users**:
    
    - Direct interaction with users who encountered the problem to gather firsthand descriptions of their experience.
        
- **Identify Symptoms**:
    
    - Carefully document the symptoms and signs of the problem as reported by users and observed in the system.
        
- **Determine if Anything has Changed**:
    
    - Investigate recent changes to the system or network environment (e.g., software updates, hardware modifications).
        
- **Duplicate the Problem, if Possible**:
    
    - Try to recreate the issue under controlled conditions to better understand it and help verify the resolution later.
        
- **Approach Multiple Problems Individually**:
    
    - If multiple issues exist, tackle them one at a time to ensure each problem is fully resolved.
        

---

### **Step 2: Establish a Theory of Probable Cause**

- **Formulate a Theory**:
    
    - Develop possible causes for the problem based on the collected information.
        
- **Question the Obvious**:
    
    - Start with simple and common causes (e.g., disconnected cables, incorrect settings).
        
- **Consider Multiple Approaches**:
    
    - Keep an open mind and consider various possible causes and solutions.
        
- **Use the OSI Model**:
    
    - Troubleshoot systematically using the OSI model, either from top to bottom or bottom to top, depending on the symptoms.
        
- **Divide and Conquer**:
    
    - Break down the problem into smaller parts to isolate and identify where the issue is occurring.
        

---

### **Step 3: Test the Theory to Determine the Cause**

- **Test the Hypothesis**:
    
    - Apply practical methods to verify whether the theory is accurate.
        
- **If the Theory is Confirmed**:
    
    - Once confirmed, determine the next steps for resolution (e.g., repairing hardware, updating software, changing configurations).
        
- **If the Theory is Not Confirmed**:
    
    - Develop a new theory or escalate the issue to higher-level support for more expertise if needed.
        

---

### 🔧 **Step 4: Establish a Plan of Action**

- **Goal**: Develop a clear, step-by-step plan to resolve the identified problem.
    
- **Considerations**:
    
    - Ensure minimal disruption to users or operations.
        
    - Account for potential impacts to other systems, services, or users.
        
    - Plan for rollback in case the fix introduces new issues.
        

---

### 🚀 **Step 5: Implement the Solution or Escalate**

- **Action**: Apply the fix as per the plan.
    
- **Escalation**:
    
    - If the issue is beyond your knowledge or authority, escalate it to a higher support tier or a subject matter expert.
        

---

### ✅ **Step 6: Verify Full System Functionality**

- **Post-Fix Testing**:
    
    - Confirm the issue is resolved and all system components are functioning normally.
        
- **Preventive Measures**:
    
    - Implement changes (patches, configurations, training) to prevent the issue from happening again.
        

---

### 📝 **Step 7: Document Everything**

- **What to Record**:
    
    - Problem description, diagnosis steps, final solution.
        
    - Any unexpected effects or workarounds.
        
    - Lessons learned for future reference.
        
- **Why**:
    
    - Builds organizational knowledge.
        
    - Helps with training and incident response in the future.
