## 🚦 **Network Performance Issues**

### 📌 **1. Congestion / Contention**

- **Cause:** Too much traffic at once; malware generating excess traffic
    
- **Fix:** Upgrade bandwidth; scan for malware; use IDS/IPS
    

---

### 🔗 **2. Bottlenecking**

- **Cause:** One slow device/link limits the whole network
    
- **Fix:** Upgrade weak links (e.g., switch/router); balance traffic load
    

---

### 📶 **3. Bandwidth**

- **Issue:** Not enough capacity for demand
    
- **Fix:** Monitor usage; apply traffic shaping & prioritization (QoS)
    

---

### 🚚 **4. Throughput Capacity**

- **Issue:** Actual speed is lower than expected
    
- **Fix:** Optimize configs; reduce interference; upgrade hardware
    

---

### ⏱️ **5. Latency**

- **Issue:** Delays in data delivery (bad for real-time apps)
    
- **Fix:** Reduce hops, use faster connections, deploy CDNs
    

---

### 📦 **6. Packet Loss**

- **Issue:** Packets get dropped → retransmissions, slow performance
    
- **Fix:** Improve hardware; scan for interference; enable error correction
    

---

### 🎙️ **7. Jitter**

- **Issue:** Uneven packet arrival times → choppy VoIP/video
    
- **Fix:** Use QoS; apply jitter buffers
    

---

## 📡 **Wireless Issues**

### 📶 **1. Wireless Interference**

- **Cause:** Nearby electronics, physical objects
    
- **Fix:** Remove/relocate interference sources; adjust channel selection
    

---

### 🔁 **2. Channel Overlap**

- **Cause:** Multiple APs using the same or overlapping channels
    
- **Fix:** Use **non-overlapping channels (1, 6, 11)**; enable auto channel selection
    

---

### 📉 **3. Signal Degradation or Loss**

- **Cause:** Distance, walls, or obstacles weaken signal
    
- **Fix:** Reposition APs; add boosters/repeaters
    

---

### 📍 **4. Insufficient Wireless Coverage**

- **Issue:** Dead zones with weak/no signal
    
- **Fix:** Conduct site surveys; deploy more APs
    

---

### 🔄 **5. Client Disassociation**

- **Issue:** Devices frequently disconnect and reconnect
    
- **Fix:** Strengthen signal; reduce interference
    

---

### 🚶 **6. Roaming Misconfiguration**

- **Issue:** Devices drop connection when moving between APs
    
- **Fix:** Optimize roaming settings to allow seamless transitions
    

---
