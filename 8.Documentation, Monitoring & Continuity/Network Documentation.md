### **Common Documentation in Networking**

Networking documentation includes **visual and written records** that support the **design, management, and troubleshooting** of network infrastructures. It ensures **clarity, consistency, and efficiency** across teams.

---

### **Types of Documentation**

#### 1. **Physical Network Diagram**

- Shows **physical layout** of devices (routers, switches, firewalls).
    
- Useful for **troubleshooting** and **hardware maintenance**.
    
    ![[Pasted image 20250419063439.png]]
    

#### 2. **Logical Network Diagram**

- Illustrates **data flow**, IP addresses, subnets, and routing.
    
- Focuses on **network design and segmentation** (not physical layout).
    
    ![[Pasted image 20250419063501.png]]
    

#### 3. **Rack Diagram**

- Displays devices in **server racks** (servers, switches, etc.).
    
- Helps with **space planning**, **cooling**, and **organization**.
    
    ![[Pasted image 20250419063518.png]]
    

#### 4. **Cable Maps/Diagrams**

- Visualize **cable paths and connections**.
    
- Important for **installation**, **troubleshooting**, and **upgrades**.
    
    ![[Pasted image 20250419063534.png]]
    

#### 5. **Network Diagrams by OSI Layer**

- **Layer 1**: Physical – cabling and device locations.
    
- **Layer 2**: Data Link – switch paths, VLANs.
    
- **Layer 3**: Network – IPs, subnets, routing protocols.

### **Asset Inventory**

#### 1. **Hardware Inventory**

- Tracks all **physical devices** (type, specs, location, condition).
    
- Supports **lifecycle management**.
    

#### 2. **Software Inventory**

- Lists all **software and configurations**.
    
- Helps with **security**, **upgrades**, and **compliance**.
    

#### **3. Licensing Management**

- Tracks software **licenses, usage rights, expirations**, and **renewals**.
    
- Ensures **legal compliance** and avoids **penalties**.
    

#### **4. Warranty & Support Management**

- Maintains records of **warranty and support contracts** for network hardware/software.
    
- Ensures **fast service claims** and **uninterrupted support**.
    

#### **5. IP Address Management (IPAM)**

- Tracks and manages **IP address allocation**.
    
- Prevents **IP conflicts** and integrates with **DHCP/DNS**.
    
- Supports **planning, compliance**, and **network reliability**.
    

#### **6. Service-Level Agreement (SLA)**

- Defines **service expectations** between providers and clients.
    
- Covers **responsibilities, performance metrics**, and **penalties** for violations.
    

#### **7. Wireless Survey & Heat Maps**

- Assesses **Wi-Fi coverage** and **performance**.
    
- Identifies optimal **access point placement**.
    
- **Heat maps** visually show signal strength for **planning improvements**.

# **Life-Cycle Management**

- Manages network devices from **purchase to disposal**.
    
- Ensures infrastructure stays **secure, efficient**, and **up-to-date**.
    

#### **1. End-of-Life (EOL)**

- Marks when a product is no longer **sold or manufactured**.
    
- Planning ahead avoids **disruptions and risks**.
    

#### **2. End-of-Support (EOS)**

- Indicates when a vendor **stops updates and support**.
    
- Essential to plan for **upgrades** to maintain **security**.
    
---
# **Software Management in Lifecycle**

- Focuses on keeping network software **updated, secure**, and **compliant**.
    
- Involves **patching, version tracking**, and **optimization**.
    

#### **1. Patches & Bug Fixes**

- Regular updates fix **vulnerabilities** and **bugs**.
    
- A good **patch management process** ensures timely updates and reduces risks.
    

#### **2. Operating System (OS) Management**

- Involves **regular updates and maintenance** of device OS.
    
- Ensures **security, performance, and feature enhancements**.
    
- Keeps network devices **stable and secure**.
    

#### **3. Firmware Updates**

- Firmware controls **hardware-level operations** in devices.
    
- Updates fix **bugs**, patch **vulnerabilities**, and may add features.
    
- Must be **planned carefully** to avoid downtime.
    
---
# **Decommissioning Network Assets**

- Safe removal of **obsolete or unused devices**.
    
- Includes **secure data erasure** and **eco-friendly disposal**.
    
- Must follow **legal and compliance guidelines**.
    
---
# **Change Management**

- A structured method to manage **network changes**.
    
- Ensures **minimal disruption**, **standardized procedures**, and **documented approvals**.
    
- Improves **efficiency and service quality**.
    

##### **→ Request Tracking / Service Request Management**

- Logs and monitors **change requests** from initiation to review.
    
- Ensures changes are **aligned with business needs** and **executed properly**.
    

#### **2. Configuration Management**

- Tracks and controls all **hardware and software settings**.
    
- Ensures **performance consistency**, **security**, and **standardization**.
    

##### **→ Production Configuration**

- Refers to the **active, live settings** in use.
    
- Must be **regularly monitored** to detect issues or unauthorized changes.
    

##### **→ Backup Configuration**

- Maintains **copies of device settings** for quick recovery.
    
- Requires **frequent updates and testing** to ensure readiness.
    

##### **→ Baseline/Golden Configuration**

- A **pre-approved template** of settings for devices.
    
- Used for **standardization**, **deployment**, and **troubleshooting**.
    
- Ensures **consistency and ease of expansion**.
