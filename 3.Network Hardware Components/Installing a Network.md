### 📌 **Importance of Installation Planning**

- Good planning = better **network performance** and **scalability**.
    
- Location of devices (like MDF/IDF) affects **accessibility**, **maintenance**, and **future upgrades**.
    

---

### 📍 **Choosing Installation Locations**

- Affects **signal quality**, **speed**, and **reliability**.
    
- Consider:
    
    - **Environment** (heat, dust, etc.)
        
    - **Distance** to users
        
    - **Safety standards**
        

---

### 🏢 **Main Distribution Frame (MDF)**

- **Central hub** for connecting external internet to internal network.
    
- Should be **centrally located** to:
    
    - Reduce cable length
        
    - Make troubleshooting easier
        
    - Support growth
        
        ![[Pasted image 20250414145130.png]]
        

---

### 🏬 **Intermediate Distribution Frame (IDF)**

- **Secondary hub** placed near users (e.g., per floor).
    
- Connects users to MDF.
    
- Improves speed and reduces lag.
    

---

### 📏 **Rack Size**

- Choose based on:
    
    - Number of devices
        
    - Space for future expansion
        
    - Available room space
        

---

### 💨 **Port-side Exhaust/Intake**

- Ensure proper **airflow** to avoid overheating.
    
- Align devices for best **ventilation** based on exhaust/intake direction.
    

---

### 🔌 **Cabling**

- Cables = the **lifelines** of the network.
    
- Use **patch panels** and **fiber panels** for neatness and easy maintenance.
    

---

### 🔄 **Patch Panels**

- Central spot to plug and manage cables.
    
- Helps with:
    
    - **Troubleshooting**
        
    - **Reconfiguration**
        
    - Reducing clutter
        

---

### 🌐 **Fiber Distribution Panels**

- Manage fiber optic cables.
    
- Ensures:
    
    - **Low signal loss**
        
    - **Efficient routing**
        
    - Central management
        

---

### 🔒 **Lockable Cabinets**

- Provide **physical security**.
    
- Protects from:
    
    - **Unauthorized access**
        
    - **Tampering or theft**
        

---

### ⚡ **Power Management**

- Keeps network **stable** and **reliable**.
    
- Plan for:
    
    - **Consistent power**
        
    - **Backup power** to avoid downtime
        

---

### 🔋 **Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS)**

- Battery backup during power loss.
    
- Key terms:
    
    - **Runtime** = how long it powers devices
        
    - **Capacity** = how much power it stores
        

---

### 🔌 **Power Distribution Units (PDUs)**

- Distribute power to multiple devices.
    
- Can be basic or **remotely monitored** for better control.
    

---

### 📊 **Managing Power Load**

- Calculate total power needed by equipment.
    
- Helps avoid **overload** and supports **future expansion**.
    

---

### ⚠️ **Voltage Considerations**

- Different devices = different **voltage needs**.
    
- Use the right power setup to avoid damage and ensure performance.
    

---

### 🌿 **Environmental Factors**

- Environment affects **lifespan and performance** of network devices.
    
- Key areas:
    
    - **Humidity**
        
    - **Temperature**
        
    - **Fire protection**
        

---

### 💧 **Humidity Control**

- Ideal range: **45–55%**.
    
- Prevents:
    
    - **Rust**
        
    - **Static electricity damage**
        

---

### 🔥 **Fire Suppression Systems**

- Use **non-water** based systems (e.g., clean agents) to protect equipment.
    
- Types:
    
    - **Wet pipe**: fast water response
        
    - **Dry pipe**: water held in tank
        
    - **Preaction**: extra trigger before water release
        
    - **Deluge**: large volume of water
        
    - **Clean agents**: safe, gas-based, no residue
        

---

### 🌡️ **Temperature Management**

- Keep environment **cool** to avoid overheating.
    
- Use **active cooling systems** (AC, fans, etc.).
